📖 About Project Nexus

Project Nexus is a next-generation attraction featuring advanced automation, multi-zone show control, and sophisticated safety systems. The attraction uses a three-PLC architecture with distributed control across Main Control, Safety Systems, and Show Effects.

👥 Key Teams

  • Control Systems Engineering: PLC programming, network infrastructure, HMI development
  • Safety Engineering: Safety system design, risk assessment, certification
  • Show Technical: Effects control, lighting, audio/video integration
  • Operations: Operator training, procedures development, daily operations
  • Maintenance: Preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, parts management
