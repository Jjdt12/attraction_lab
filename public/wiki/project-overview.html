Project Nexus Overview

Last updated: November 10, 2025 | Owner: Project Management

Executive Summary

Project Nexus represents a groundbreaking advancement in theme park attraction control systems. This dark ride combines cutting-edge automation with traditional storytelling to create an immersive guest experience.

Key Features

  • Three-PLC distributed architecture for enhanced reliability
  • 27-position track with 9 show scenes
  • Advanced safety systems with SIL-2 certification target
  • Real-time show synchronization across all effects
  • Modern web-based HMI for operator control
