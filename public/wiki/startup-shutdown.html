Startup & Shutdown Procedures

Last updated: November 1, 2025 | Owner: Operations Team | Version: 2.2

Daily Startup Procedure

Step 1: Physical Inspection (15 min)
  1. Walk entire track looking for debris or damage
  2. Check vehicle restraints and sensors
  3. Verify all emergency exits clear
  4. Inspect safety gates operation
  5. Check warning signage visible
Step 2: Power-Up Sequence (5 min)
  1. Turn on main breakers in electrical room
  2. Power on control cabinet
  3. Wait for all 3 PLC green lights
  4. Verify HMI shows "SYSTEM READY"
  5. Check Multi-PLC Status all green
Step 3: Safety System Test (10 min)
  1. Press E-STOP - verify immediate shutdown
  2. Reset E-Stop, confirm return to ready
  3. Test safety gate - should prevent start when open
  4. Verify all 9 events enabled
  5. Check brake pressure 1150-1250 PSI
Step 4: Test Cycle (5 min)
  1. Run empty vehicle test
  2. Observe smooth acceleration & braking
  3. Listen for unusual noises
  4. Verify all effects trigger correctly
  5. Confirm vehicle returns to station
⚠️ Do Not Proceed If: Any test fails, unusual noises heard, safety systems not functioning, or if you're unsure about any reading. Contact maintenance immediately.

Normal Shutdown Procedure

  1. Complete all rides in progress
  2. Click RESET RIDE to return to IDLE
  3. Turn Master Enable to OFF
  4. Document any issues in operations log
  5. Lock control cabinet
  6. Turn off main breakers
  7. Complete end-of-day checklist

Emergency Shutdown

Press Emergency Stop If:
  • Guest attempting to exit moving vehicle
  • Loose article on track
  • Unusual mechanical sounds
  • Any safety concern
  1. Press red E-STOP button
  2. System halts within 2 seconds
  3. Make PA announcement to guests
  4. Assess situation
  5. Contact maintenance if needed
  6. Document incident
