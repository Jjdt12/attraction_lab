System Change Requests

Last updated: November 12, 2025 | Owner: Rachel Park, Project Manager | CR Process v2.1
📋 Change Request Process: All modifications to the Nexus control system must be submitted through the formal CR process. This ensures proper review, testing, and documentation of all system changes.

Change Request Workflow

The Project Nexus change management process follows a structured workflow to ensure all modifications are properly evaluated for safety, functionality, and operational impact:

  1. Submission: Requester completes CR form with detailed description and justification
  2. Initial Review: Project Manager reviews for completeness and assigns CR number
  3. Technical Assessment: Engineering team evaluates feasibility and impact
  4. Safety Review: Safety team assesses any safety implications
  5. Approval: Change Control Board reviews and approves/rejects
  6. Implementation: Approved changes scheduled and executed
  7. Verification: Testing confirms change works as intended
  8. Documentation: All documentation updated to reflect changes
  9. Closure: CR formally closed with lessons learned

Change Categories & Guidelines

Category 1: Critical Safety Changes

Any change affecting safety systems, emergency stops, interlocks, or failsafe behavior. Requires full Safety Team review and may require recertification. Typical approval time: 4-6 weeks.

Category 2: Control Logic Changes

Modifications to PLC ladder logic, state machines, or timing sequences. Requires engineering review, simulation testing, and operational approval. Typical approval time: 2-3 weeks.

Category 3: Effects & Show Changes

Updates to lighting, audio, or special effects timing and parameters. Requires creative approval and show director sign-off. Typical approval time: 1-2 weeks.

Category 4: Documentation & UI Changes

Updates to HMI displays, documentation, or operator procedures. Requires operations team review. Typical approval time: 3-5 days.

⚠️ Emergency Changes: In case of safety-critical issues or system failures, emergency changes may be implemented immediately with retrospective CR documentation. All emergency changes must be reported to the Safety Manager within 24 hours.

Submitting a Change Request

To submit a new change request:

  1. Complete the CR Form available from the Project Management Office
  2. Include detailed description of proposed change and justification
  3. Identify affected systems (Main PLC, Safety PLC, Effects PLC, HMI, etc.)
  4. Estimate implementation effort and timeline
  5. Attach any supporting documentation, diagrams, or test data
  6. Email completed form to rachel.park@nexus-project.internal
