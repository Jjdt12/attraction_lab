Emergency Procedures

Last updated: October 15, 2025 | Owner: Marcus Johnson

Emergency Stop Activation

  1. Press nearest red E-STOP button immediately
  2. System will halt all motion within 2 seconds
  3. Make PA announcement: "Please remain seated"
  4. Radio control room: "E-Stop activated at Nexus"
  5. Assess guest safety
  6. Do not reset until situation resolved

Guest Evacuation

Evacuate guests if:

  • Vehicle stuck >15 minutes
  • Smoke or fire detected
  • Medical emergency requiring immediate access
  • Building evacuation alarm sounds

Evacuation Steps:

  1. Press E-STOP
  2. Call for evacuation team (ext. 4000)
  3. Use emergency walkways to reach guests
  4. Assist guests to nearest exit
  5. Account for all guests at assembly point
  6. Do not reset system until all clear

Medical Emergency

  1. Press E-STOP
  2. Call emergency services: ext. 911
  3. Provide first aid if qualified
  4. Clear path for paramedics
  5. Document incident

Fire Emergency

  1. Activate fire alarm pull station
  2. Press E-STOP
  3. Evacuate all guests immediately
  4. Call fire department: ext. 911
  5. Use fire extinguisher only if safe
  6. Evacuate to assembly point

Power Failure

  1. Emergency lights activate automatically
  2. UPS provides 30 min backup power
  3. Initiate graceful shutdown if power not restored in 10 min
  4. Assist guests using emergency lighting
  5. Wait for power restoration or evacuate
Emergency Contacts:
Park Emergency: ext. 911
Control Room: ext. 4500
Safety Officer: ext. 4502
Maintenance Emergency: Radio Ch. 2
