Project Nexus Timeline

Last updated: November 12, 2025 | Owner: Rachel Park, Project Manager
📅 Project Schedule: Project Nexus began in January 2024 with an ambitious 24-month timeline to design, build, and commission a state-of-the-art dark ride attraction. Below is a comprehensive timeline of all major milestones.

Project Phases Overview

Phase Start Date End Date Duration Status
Concept & Planning Jan 2024 Mar 2024 3 months Complete
Design & Engineering Apr 2024 Jun 2024 3 months Complete

Detailed Timeline

✓ Phase 1: Concept & Planning (Jan-Mar 2024)

  • Jan 15, 2024: Project kickoff meeting. Team assembled, scope defined
  • Jan 30, 2024: Feasibility study completed. Three-PLC architecture recommended
  • Feb 14, 2024: Budget approved ($4.2M). Timeline established
  • Feb 28, 2024: Site survey completed. Facilities requirements documented
  • Mar 15, 2024: Final concept design approved by stakeholders
  • Mar 31, 2024: Phase 1 complete. Ready to begin detailed design

✓ Phase 2: Design & Engineering (Apr-Jun 2024)

  • Apr 10, 2024: Control system architecture finalized. Modbus TCP selected
  • Apr 25, 2024: Show scene layouts and effects designed
  • May 8, 2024: Safety system design completed. SIL-2 target confirmed
  • May 22, 2024: Network topology and infrastructure plan approved
  • Jun 5, 2024: PLC I/O requirements documented. 33 coils, 10 registers per PLC
  • Jun 20, 2024: All design drawings and specifications completed
  • Jun 30, 2024: Design review passed. Engineering phase complete

✓ Phase 3: Procurement & Fabrication (Jul-Aug 2024)

  • Jul 8, 2024: OpenPLC hardware procured. Industrial PCs ordered
  • Jul 15, 2024: Network equipment received. Managed switches, cabling
  • Jul 25, 2024: Ride track and vehicle system fabrication begun
  • Aug 1, 2024: Show effects equipment ordered (lights, audio, effects)
  • Aug 12, 2024: All control cabinet fabrication completed
  • Aug 30, 2024: Final equipment delivered to site

✓ Phase 4: Installation (Aug-Sep 2024)

  • Aug 15, 2024: Control cabinet installation begun
  • Aug 22, 2024: Network infrastructure installed. All cabling pulled
  • Sep 3, 2024: Three PLCs mounted, powered, and networked
  • Sep 10, 2024: Field I/O wiring completed. All sensors connected
  • Sep 20, 2024: Show effects installation complete (lighting, audio, etc.)
  • Sep 30, 2024: Installation phase complete. Ready for programming

✓ Phase 5: Programming & Integration (Sep-Nov 2024)

  • Oct 2, 2024: PLC programming begun. Ladder logic development started
  • Oct 15, 2024: Main PLC state machine programmed and tested
  • Oct 22, 2024: Safety PLC interlock logic completed
  • Oct 29, 2024: Effects PLC show sequence programming finished
  • Nov 5, 2024: Multi-PLC communication tested. Handshaking verified
  • Nov 12, 2024: HMI developed and connected to all three PLCs
  • Nov 20, 2024: First successful end-to-end ride cycle completed
  • Nov 30, 2024: Programming phase complete. Ready for testing

▶ Phase 6: Testing & Commissioning (Nov 2024-Dec 2025) - CURRENT

  • Dec 2024 - Jan 2025: Component-level testing. Individual system validation
  • Feb - Apr 2025: Integration testing. Multi-system coordination verified
  • May - Jul 2025: Operational testing. Hundreds of ride cycles completed
  • Aug - Oct 2025: Endurance testing. 24/7 operation for weeks at a time
  • Nov 2025: Final refinements based on testing data. Performance optimization
  • Dec 2025: Pre-certification testing. Final preparations for safety audit

Current Status (Nov 12, 2025): Testing is 90% complete. Over 5,000 successful ride cycles logged. System performing excellently with 99.7% uptime.

◆ Phase 7: Safety Certification (Dec 2025-Jan 2026) - UPCOMING

  • Dec 2-4, 2025: External safety auditors on-site for SIL-2 assessment
  • Dec 9-13, 2025: Safety documentation review and interviews
  • Dec 16-20, 2025: Witness testing of all safety-critical functions
  • Jan 6, 2026: Final certification report expected
  • Jan 13, 2026: Address any findings or recommendations
  • Jan 20, 2026: Certificate of Compliance issued (target date)

◆ Phase 8: Grand Opening (Feb 2026)

  • Jan 25, 2026: Soft opening for VIP guests and stakeholders
  • Feb 1-7, 2026: Technical rehearsals with limited guest access
  • Feb 10-14, 2026: Cast member previews and final training
  • Feb 18, 2026: Official grand opening ceremony
  • Feb 19, 2026: Public operations begin
