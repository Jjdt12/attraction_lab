Safety Systems Documentation

Last updated: October 20, 2025 | Owner: Marcus Johnson, Safety Officer | Version: 3.0
⚠️ Critical: Safety systems must NEVER be bypassed or disabled. Any modification requires Safety Engineering approval and re-certification.

Safety PLC Architecture

The dedicated Safety PLC (Port 503) monitors all safety-critical functions independently from the Main control PLC. It can override ride operation at any time.

Key Safety Features

  • Emergency Stop System: Hardwired E-Stop buttons at 4 locations
  • Safety Gates: Magnetic safety switches on loading gates
  • Zone Occupancy: Infrared sensors detect vehicle position
  • Speed Monitoring: Independent speed encoder verification
  • Brake Monitoring: Pressure sensors + position switches
  • Watchdog Timer: Resets system if PLC stops responding

Emergency Stop Response

When E-Stop activated:

  1. Motor power cut within 50ms
  2. Friction brakes engage within 200ms
  3. All show effects disable immediately
  4. Alarm sounds in control room
  5. System requires manual reset

Fail-Safe Principles

  • Loss of power = brakes engage (spring-applied, electrically released)
  • Loss of signal = system interprets as fault
  • All safety inputs normally closed (NC) contacts
  • Redundant sensors on critical functions
  • Safety PLC uses watchdog timer

SIL Rating

System targets SIL-2 (Safety Integrity Level 2) certification per IEC 61508:

  • Probability of dangerous failure: 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁷ per hour
  • Independent safety assessment completed August 2025
  • Annual safety audit required
  • All safety components must be SIL-rated

Maintenance Requirements

  • Daily: E-Stop test, gate interlock test
  • Weekly: Brake pressure check, sensor alignment
  • Monthly: Full safety system functional test
  • Annual: Third-party safety audit & certification
